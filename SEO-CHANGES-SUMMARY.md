## Why These Changes Matter

### 1. Search Intent Matching
**Users search:** "entradas bts peru"  
**Not:** "estadio san marcos"

Leading with the ticket word matches what people are looking for.

### 2. Availability Signals Drive Clicks
**Research shows:** Adding "Disponibles" / "Disponíveis" increases CTR by 8-15%

**Why it works:**
- Reduces uncertainty (are tickets still available?)
- Creates urgency (available NOW, not sold out)
- Matches transactional intent (ready to buy)

### 3. Optimal Title Length
**Google's behavior:**
- 51-60 characters: ~40% rewrite rate ✅
- Under 20 characters: 95% rewrite rate ❌
- Over 60 characters: Gets truncated ❌

All titles now in the sweet spot where Google is least likely to rewrite them.

### 4. Keyword Front-Loading
Search engines give more weight to words at the beginning of the title.

**Priority order:**
1. Ticket word (Entradas/Boletos/Ingressos)
2. Artist/Event (BTS)
3. Country (Perú/México/Chile/etc)
4. Year (2026)
5. Availability (Disponibles)
6. Venue (Estadio San Marcos/etc)
